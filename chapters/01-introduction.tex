\section{Research problem and questions}

The gap in existing research, stated as one problem.

\begin{enumerate}[label=\textsf{\textbf{RQ\arabic*}}, leftmargin=4em, align=left]
  \item How do states reach provider-less infrastructure through its
    developers, and how accurately do public accounts, LLM answers among
    them, describe that reach? Answered in Publication~I.\label{rq:1}
  \item How do the people who run Nostr relays weigh the benefits and harms
    that pass through them, and what do they do when harmful content arrives?
    Answered in Publication~II.\label{rq:2}
  \item How do developers of non-custodial privacy software read and respond
    to prosecutions such as those of Samourai Wallet and Tornado Cash?
    Answered in Publication~III.\label{rq:3}
\end{enumerate}
